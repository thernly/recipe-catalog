# Meal Planning User Guide

## Overview

The Weekly Meal Planning feature allows you to organize your household's meals for the week. You can plan breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, and other meals by selecting recipes from your catalog and scheduling them across a 7-day calendar.

## Key Features

- **Weekly Calendar View**: Visual grid showing all meals for the week (Monday through Sunday)
- **Multiple Meal Types**: Plan for breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, and other meals
- **Recipe Integration**: Select any recipe from your household's catalog
- **Serving Adjustments**: Specify the number of servings for each planned meal
- **Notes**: Add notes to any planned meal (e.g., "Make ahead", "Leftovers from Tuesday")
- **Multiple Recipes per Slot**: Add multiple recipes to the same day and meal type
- **Household Sharing**: All household members can view and edit the shared meal plan
- **Week Navigation**: Easily navigate between past, current, and future weeks

## Getting Started

### Accessing Meal Planning

1. Log in to your Recipe Catalog account
2. Navigate to the **Meal Planning** section from the main menu
3. You'll see the current week's meal plan by default

### Understanding the Calendar Grid

The meal planning calendar is organized as:
- **Rows**: Days of the week (Monday through Sunday)
- **Columns**: Meal types (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack)
- **Cells**: Individual meal slots where you can add recipes

## Planning Your Meals

### Adding a Meal to the Plan

1. Click on any empty cell or the **"Add Meal"** button
2. In the dialog that opens:
   - **Select Day**: Choose the day of the week
   - **Select Meal Type**: Choose breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack, or other
   - **Select Recipe**: Search for and select a recipe from your catalog
   - **Set Servings** (optional): Specify how many servings you plan to make
   - **Add Notes** (optional): Include any helpful notes
3. Click **"Save"** to add the meal to your plan

### Editing a Planned Meal

1. Click on an existing meal in the calendar
2. The edit dialog will open with the current details
3. Modify any field:
   - Change the recipe
   - Adjust the day or meal type
   - Update servings
   - Edit or add notes
4. Click **"Save"** to update the meal

### Removing a Planned Meal

1. Click on the meal you want to remove
2. In the edit dialog, click the **"Delete"** button
3. Confirm the deletion when prompted

### Adding Multiple Recipes to One Meal

You can add multiple recipes to the same meal slot (useful for multi-course meals or side dishes):

1. Add the first recipe to a meal slot as usual
2. Click on the same meal slot again
3. Click **"Add Another Recipe"** or the **"+"** button
4. Select the additional recipe and save
5. Both recipes will now appear in that meal slot

## Navigating Between Weeks

### View Different Weeks

Use the navigation controls at the top of the calendar:
- **Previous Week**: Click the left arrow or "Previous" button
- **Next Week**: Click the right arrow or "Next" button
- **Current Week**: Click "Today" or "Current Week" to jump back to the present

### Week Display

The current week being displayed is shown prominently at the top:
- Example: "Week of January 6, 2025"
- The current week (containing today) is highlighted

## Tips and Best Practices

### Weekly Planning Workflow

1. **Review Your Schedule**: Check your weekly calendar for busy days
2. **Check Your Pantry**: Note what ingredients you already have
3. **Plan Around Leftovers**: Schedule recipes that make good leftovers for busy days
4. **Balance Variety**: Mix different cuisines and cooking methods throughout the week
5. **Consider Prep Time**: Schedule quick meals on busy nights, elaborate meals when you have time

### Efficient Planning

- **Plan Similar Meals Together**: Use ingredients across multiple recipes to reduce waste
- **Batch Cooking**: Schedule recipes that can be doubled and frozen
- **Theme Nights**: Assign themes to different nights (e.g., "Meatless Monday", "Taco Tuesday")
- **Use Notes**: Add reminders like "Thaw chicken" or "Prep vegetables the night before"

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I plan meals for multiple weeks?

Yes! Navigate to any future week and start planning. You can plan as far ahead as you like.

### What happens to past meal plans?

Past meal plans are preserved and can be viewed for reference. This is useful for:
- Remembering what you cooked
- Repeating successful weekly plans
- Tracking dietary patterns

### Can I copy a meal plan to another week?

This feature is planned for a future update. For now, you can manually add the same recipes to different weeks.

### Can I plan more than one recipe for a meal?

Yes! You can add multiple recipes to the same day and meal type. This is useful for side dishes, multi-course meals, or when serving different options.

### Can I delete an entire week's plan at once?

Yes, use the **"Delete Plan"** button at the top of the calendar to remove all meals for the current week.

### Do I need to specify servings?

No, servings are optional. However, specifying servings helps with:
- Shopping list generation (upcoming feature)
- Ingredient quantity calculations
- Meal preparation planning

### Can I plan meals from recipes in other households?

No, you can only use recipes from your own household's catalog. This ensures privacy and data isolation between households.

### What if I delete a recipe that's in my meal plan?

If a recipe is soft-deleted (moved to trash), it will still appear in your meal plan but may be marked as unavailable. You should remove it and select a different recipe.

## Troubleshooting

### I can't see my meal plan

- Ensure you're logged in and have joined or created a household
- Check that you're viewing the correct week
- Try refreshing the page

### Changes aren't saving

- Check your internet connection
- Ensure you have permission to edit the household meal plan
- Try logging out and back in

### The wrong week is displayed

- Use the "Current Week" or "Today" button to return to the present week
- Check that your device's date and time settings are correct
